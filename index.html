About me

I am a second-year Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Michigan, advised by Prof. Z. Morley Mao. I obtained my undergraduate degree in Computer Science and Data Science from the University of Michigan. My research spans efficient machine learning systems and their applications, including language models, multimodal models, and intelligent agents.
